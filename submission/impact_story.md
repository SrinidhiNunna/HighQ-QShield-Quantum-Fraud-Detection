# Real World Impact

Mobile money systems are increasingly used by underserved populations who lack access to traditional banking infrastructure.

Fraudulent transactions can significantly impact trust in digital financial services and lead to direct financial losses.

This project explores whether quantum machine learning techniques can contribute to earlier and more accurate fraud detection in mobile money ecosystems.

Potential beneficiaries:

- Microfinance institutions
- Digital wallet providers
- Underserved communities
- Financial inclusion initiatives
